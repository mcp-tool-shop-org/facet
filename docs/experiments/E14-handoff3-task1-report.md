## 1. The chroma floor — and the prediction it falsified

The dispatch required the floor to come from **the separation structure of the pair's realised
values**, never from where L1's 5.39 cast falls. I predicted (F1/F3) a wide empty gap between
an achromatic mass and a coloured mass, with the floor landing in it.

**There is no gap. The chroma density is monotone.** Over 89,876 figure pixels, pooled across
both accepted views:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

It falls smoothly from the peak and **flattens into a tail of ~250–350 px per 0.5 bin that runs

flattest point of a monotone decay, not a valley, and reporting it as an antimode would be the
exact error this repo has already paid for once (*distant medians do not imply a gap between
them*).

**What does exist is a KNEE.** The decay stops and the flat tail begins at **C\* 11.5–13** —
where two processes cross over: the achromatic mass's tail below, the genuinely coloured
materials above.

**Proposed floor: 12.0, on the knee.** Stated plainly: **12.0 is also the value W3 and the
galleon inherited**, so this is an inherited number vindicated by this subject's own data, not
a new derivation, and it is not dressed as a discovery.

**Where L1's cast falls is a RESULT and F2 held:** L1's realised body chroma is **2.93** (view

## 2. The band edges — the rule, stated before it was applied

> A band is the contiguous span of **BODY** support (silhouette depth > 2 px, so rim mixing
> cannot vote on a material's edges) around a local body peak, trimmed where body density falls
> below **1% of that band's own peak**. Per-band normalisation, because gold's peak is 58% of
> wine's and a global cut would trim the smaller band harder.

Body peaks found rather than assumed: **hue 11 (755 px), 75 (436 px), 303 (51 px)**.

| trim | wine | gold | lavender |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1% — THE RULE AS STATED** | **332–32 (60°)**, 6,039 body / 658 rim | **42–104 (62°)**, 3,137 body / 55 rim | **292–314 (22°)**, 183 body / **2,366 rim** |
| 2% | 350–30 (40°) | 58–102 (44°) | 292–314 |
| 5% | 354–26 (32°) | 64–102 (38°) | 294–314 |
| 10% | 356–24 (28°) | 68–100 (32°) | 300–314 |

The sensitivity is reported because **a band edge is where a derivation stops being a
measurement and becomes a convention** — the galleon said so about its blue band and the
discipline is kept. **I did not switch rules after seeing the result.** At 2% the two material
bands tighten by 20° each and the trough between them opens from 6° to 24°; that trade is the
ruling's to make, not mine.

**Forbidden span with all three bands: 216° = 60.0% of the hue circle** (galleon 80.0%, W3
47.2%). The entire green–cyan–blue arc **105–291, 187°, carries zero body pixels**.

backdrop's hue*, which would make the gem's drift and L1's cast one phenomenon. The gem's view-1

## 4. The lavender question — settled by PLACEMENT, not by hue

**The mechanism first.** `palette_gate.py` measures only inside the exact silhouette, so the
realised backdrop cannot enter as background — it enters as **antialiased rim pixels carrying
backdrop colour**. On the beast the backdrop sat below the floor and never reached the hue gate
(the 15i mechanism); this subject's backdrop is at C\* 32.6–37.1, far above any floor.

**Hue cannot separate rim mixing from a material. Depth can, and it was measured directly
rather than proxied:**

| population (C\* ≥ 12) | px | **within 2 px of the rim** | median depth | median row |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| all figure | 12,686 | 25.9% | 7.69 px | 0.258 |
| **hue 290–310** | 2,416 | **93.1%** | **1.00 px** | 0.470 |
| hue 300–310 | 2,276 | **93.7%** | 1.00 px | 0.506 |
| **wine 0–25** | 6,243 | **8.0%** | 7.99 px | 0.212 |
| **gold 60–95** | 2,852 | **0.3%** | 16.29 px | 0.301 |

**Declared materials live deep inside the figure; the lavender population lives on its edge.**
Its deep remnant is 144 px = 0.160% of the figure, spread from row 0.13 to 0.91 — *not*
concentrated at the pommel, so it is not the gem.

**The 295-vs-305 cut the dispatch named as the hardest question is answered, and not the way I
predicted.** There is no antimode in hue — the figure density rises monotonically from 4 px at
292 to 459 px at 305 and falls away, one population. **But the cut does not need one**: L1's
~295 cast sits at C\* 2.93–5.39, *below the 12.0 floor*, so it never reaches the hue gate at
all. **The two are separated by CHROMA and by PLACEMENT, not by hue.** B3 predicted a
suspension and a suspension is proposed — but for a different reason than predicted: not
*"no boundary exists"* but *"this is not a material and a material band must not pretend it
is one."*

**The band is proposed SUSPENDED, and the run reports both ways** — the galleon's blue-band
precedent, followed exactly.

demonstrated rather than asserted — and it is the honest answer to the dispatch's validation

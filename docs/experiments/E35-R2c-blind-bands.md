## The mechanism reasoning behind my fork call, stated before the run

For init-bleed-through: the clay init **is** the pale thing — task 0a sampled `armclay_1.png`
at RGB 177–222, achromatic, **L\* 72–88** — and 6d measured the pale regions at **L\* 76.54**,

anchor, and 6e found smooth lobes rather than step edges.

**Against it, and I am registering the objection rather than burying it:** the clay is
C\* ≈ 0.9, so *pure* bleed-through predicts a chroma collapse. 6d measured only a **9.48%**
chroma drop with **zero** pale pixels below the C\* 8.0 floor. A pure init showing through
should have desaturated far harder. So if the cn arms come back unchanged, the honest reading
is "init-**influenced**, not raw init bleed" — and I say so now rather than after.

## The delegation — one question, and it is a real anomaly

**Why is our atlas ~9× worse than the published xatlas baseline for our own mesh class?**

PartUV's Trellis row puts xatlas at **1,541.6 charts average, 895 median**. Our mesh produces
**14,010** islands raw (9,166 after culling faces no camera sees). Same library, same broad mesh
family, an order of magnitude apart. That gap is not explained by "auto-unwrap is imperfect," and
if it has a cheap upstream cause we would rather fix that than chase unwrappers.

What we can tell you about how the mesh is made:

- **TRELLIS.2, `1024_cascade`** reconstruction from a single concept image, run locally.
- Its `to_glb` **decimates**, and **the UVs are generated by xatlas inside that pipeline**. Our own
  decimation step then **carries those existing UVs through the cut** rather than re-unwrapping
  after. A prior research pass flagged that as the reverse of every documented pipeline; we have
  never tested it.
- Final mesh: **287,170 faces**. Median island 102 texels on a 4096 atlas.
- We know of **1,035 zero-UV-area faces** that our decimation tool cannot see.
- On other subjects in this family we measured the reconstructions to be **hollow, double-walled
  shells** — walls roughly two voxels around an empty cavity, confirmed three independent ways.
  Whether that holds for this character is **unmeasured**; our topology instrument declines to
  compute on character meshes because it needs a second manifold piece above 1% of faces and the
  largest runs 98.2–98.6%.
- The mesh is not welded before decimation in any step we can point to — and an exported glTF
  splits a vertex at every UV seam.

**Specifically:** does unwrapping *before* decimation and dragging the UVs through the cut plausibly
multiply chart count like this? Would a hidden inner wall be atlased as its own chart set, roughly
doubling everything? Do the split vertices at UV seams survive as separate components and get
counted as charts? Which of these would you test first, and what is the cheapest measurement that
distinguishes them?
